# SANG

### Connective Intelligence for People, Problems, Resources and Opportunities

> Don't make people enter the network. Bring the network to the person.

SANG is a connective intelligence layer that makes the relationships between people, problems, skills, resources and opportunities visible — and helps turn those connections into action.

---

## What SANG Does

SANG understands a person's intent and routes them toward the right type of connection.

Instead of forcing users to search through disconnected systems, SANG identifies:

- People who can help
- Problems that need attention
- Relevant resources
- Available opportunities
- Local connections
- Emergency services

---

## 333 Access Layer

SANG introduces a 333-style access layer designed around three simple routes:

### INFORMATION

For users looking for knowledge.

SANG provides relevant information, resources and opportunities.

### ASSISTANCE

For users who have a problem and need a connection.

SANG identifies relevant local helpers, resources and opportunities.

### EMERGENCY

For urgent situations.

SANG immediately escalates the user to the appropriate emergency service instead of continuing unnecessary conversation.

---

## India-First Intelligence

The current prototype is designed for India, with location-aware routing demonstrated in Gujarat.

USER
  ↓
LANGUAGE
  ↓
