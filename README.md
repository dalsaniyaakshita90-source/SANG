# SANG

### Connective Intelligence for People, Problems, Resources and Opportunities

> Don't make people enter the network. Bring the network to the person.

SANG is a connective intelligence layer that makes the relationships between people, problems, skills, resources and opportunities visible — and helps turn those connections into action.

---

## What SANG Does

SANG understands a person's intent and routes them toward the right type of connection.

Instead of forcing users to search through disconnected systems, SANG identifies:

- People who can help
- Problems that need attention
- Relevant resources
- Available opportunities
- Local connections
- Emergency services

The goal is not simply to provide information.

The goal is to connect the right person, problem, resource or opportunity at the right time.

---

## 333 Access Layer

SANG introduces a 333-style access layer designed around three simple routes:

### INFORMATION

For users looking for knowledge.

SANG provides relevant information, resources and available opportunities.

### ASSISTANCE

For users who have a problem and need a connection.

SANG identifies relevant local helpers, resources and opportunities.

### EMERGENCY

For urgent situations.

SANG immediately escalates the user to the appropriate emergency service instead of continuing unnecessary conversation.

The current 333 layer is a web-based prototype and simulation. It is not a real telecom dialable 333 number.

---

## India-First Intelligence

The current prototype is designed for India, with location-aware routing demonstrated through Gujarat-based examples.

Emergency routing uses India-specific rules:

- Medical emergencies → 108
- Fire emergencies → 101
- Police / general emergency → 112

The architecture is designed so that emergency rules can become country-specific rather than relying on globally hardcoded assumptions.

SANG's prototype brings together several intelligence layers:

- People matching
- Problem matching
- Resource matching
- Opportunity matching
- Location-aware routing
- Agriculture intelligence
- Multilingual interaction
- Voice input and output
- Emergency routing
- Trust and verification
- Safe no-match handling

## Multilingual Access

The current prototype supports interaction across multiple Indian languages, including:

- English
- Hindi
- Gujarati
- Marathi
- Bengali
- Tamil
- Telugu
- Kannada
- Malayalam
- Punjabi

Selected romanized conversational input is also supported.

The language layer is designed to help users interact naturally before routing their request into the relevant SANG pathway.
