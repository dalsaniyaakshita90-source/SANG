\# SANG — World Bank Small AI Use Case



\## Sector

Agriculture



\## Core Problem



Smallholder farmers often struggle to find the right person, resource,

service, opportunity, or support for a specific agricultural problem.



Information may exist, but it is fragmented across people, organizations,

government resources, local knowledge, and services.



The problem is not simply lack of information.



The problem is:



WHO can help?

WHAT resource is relevant?

WHERE is it available?

WHY is it relevant to this specific situation?



\## Proposed Solution



SANG for Agriculture is a lightweight matching system that connects:



Farmers

↓

Agricultural problems

↓

Relevant people / experts / organizations

↓

Relevant resources / schemes / services

↓

Actionable opportunities



based on context such as:



\- problem

\- skills

\- agricultural category

\- location

\- available resources

\- eligibility

\- relevant opportunities



\## Small AI Principle



The system should be designed for constrained environments.



It should prioritize:



\- lightweight models

\- structured local data

\- low-bandwidth interaction

\- simple text-based interaction

\- explainable matches

\- localized information

\- graceful handling of incomplete information



\## Example Query



"I am a small farmer in Gujarat facing an agriculture problem.

Who or what resource can help me?"



SANG should return:



1\. Relevant people / organizations

2\. Relevant resources

3\. Relevant opportunities

4\. Why each result was matched

5\. What information is missing, if any



\## What Makes SANG Different



SANG does not simply answer a question.



It builds a contextual connection between:



PERSON + PROBLEM + RESOURCE + OPPORTUNITY



and explains why the connection exists.



\## Prototype Goal



Build a working small-AI prototype demonstrating one narrow

agricultural use case rather than attempting to solve agriculture broadly.
