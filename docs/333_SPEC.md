\# SANG — 333 Access Layer Specification



\## Purpose



333 is SANG's low-access entry point for people who may not have a

smartphone, app, website, or high digital comfort.



A user can access SANG through a simple phone pathway.



\## Core Routes



333 supports three primary routes:



\### 1. INFORMATION



Used when a person needs information.



Examples:



\- Nearest government hospital

\- Relevant agricultural information

\- Government scheme information

\- Market or service information



\### 2. ASSISTANCE



Used when a person needs help completing or accessing something.



Examples:



\- Help obtaining medicine

\- Finding an appropriate local service

\- Connecting with a relevant person or organization

\- Accessing an opportunity or resource



\### 3. EMERGENCY



Used when immediate assistance is required.



Emergency cases should not depend on a long AI conversation.



SANG should prioritize:



\- Immediate routing

\- Appropriate emergency services

\- Human escalation



\## Language



333 should support interaction that adapts to the user's language and

conversational style.



The system may offer a simple choice to continue or switch language.



The underlying opportunity, service, or resource should not disappear

because the user speaks a different language.



\## Accessibility



333 is designed for keypad-phone access and should minimize:



\- Complex interfaces

\- Long menus

\- High digital literacy requirements

\- Smartphone dependency



\## Safety



SANG should not pretend to resolve a situation when it cannot safely

understand or resolve it.



If confidence is insufficient:



1\. Stop automated resolution.

2\. Explain that human assistance is required.

3\. Escalate to an appropriate human or official channel.

\## Core Principle



Don't make people enter the network.



Bring the network to the person.
